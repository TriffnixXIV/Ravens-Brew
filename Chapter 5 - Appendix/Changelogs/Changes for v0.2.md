#### [[Action Economy]]
- every turn, in addition to a Reaction, you also have one Free Reaction that can be used for some stuff.
#### [[Perception & Focus]]
- made the wording more precise.
- _Switch Focus_ now takes a Free Reaction instead of a Reaction.
- _Switch Focus_ can also be used as a Free Action.
- added _Draw Focus_ so you can draw the focus of a creature somewhere else through various means.
- added _Losing Focus_ so you now lose focus under certain circumstances.
#### [[AC-less Defence]]
- Damage Reduction now only reduces non-spell damage.
- cleaned up wording for Damage Reduction and Shields.
#### [[Blocking]]
- added a _Blocking Bonus_ to add to a blocking ability check equal to 8 + either Strength mod or Dexmod + Proficiency (if proficient with shields).
- limited the uses of the _Defensive Stance_ & _Protective Stance_ to the Proficiency Bonus.
#### [[Dodging]]
- limited the uses of the _Evasive Stance_ to the Proficiency Bonus.
#### Variable attack actions
- tried to add them, but the PHB was faster.
