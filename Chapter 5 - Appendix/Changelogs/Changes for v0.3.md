#### General
- reordered the chapters to make more sense.
#### [[Action Economy]]
- _Reserving Actions_ now only gives one Reaction per Action.
#### [[Dodging]]
- added elaboration on the idea.
- added a _Dodging Bonus_ to add to a dodging ability check equal to 5 + either Athletics or Acrobatics.
- increased the distance of a _Dodge & Roll_ to 10 feet.
#### [[Parrying]]
- added so you can now use a reaction to parry, nice.
- uses a _Parrying Bonus_ to add to a parrying ability check equal to 5 + attack bonus of the parry weapon.
- _Parry_ allows you to parry a melee attack, potentially reducing it's damage to 0.
- _Parrying Stance_ allows you to parry more.
- reworked the Battle Masters _Parry_ to fit.
- adjusted existing Monster Parries accordingly.
#### [[Surge Dice]]
- usage needn't be announced at the start of an action anymore.
- removed _Regeneration_.
- reintroduced _Tenacity_.
- _Tenacity_ now only gives temporary HP until the start of your next turn.
- _Speed_ no longer scales with the roll and now gives 10ft. of movement speed per die spent on this option.
- added _Skill_ to add the roll to any one roll you make, replacing multiple other previous options.
- changed _Last Stand_ to _Dying Struggle_ and made it only trigger once when dropping below 25% maxHP.
- changed _Elation_ to trigger after applying the effects of the attack.
