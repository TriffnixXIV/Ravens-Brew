The introduction of [[Spell Points]] has made it easier to create features that interact with the cost of spells. Some such features can be found in the list of [[Caster Training]] features, including some that reduce the [[Spell Points|Spell Point]] cost for selected spellcasts. This can lead to the [[Spell Points|Spell Point]] cost dropping below 0, making rules that interact with this possible.

Additionally, the removal of automatic cantrip scaling has decreased the power of casters as soon as their [[Spell Points]] run out, especially affecting Warlocks. This incentivizes the introduction of another way to make cantrips more powerful without [[Spell Points|Spell Point]] cost. As upcasting is the usual way to power up a spell, another version of upcasting makes intuitive and mechanical sense.

The following rules consequently merge upcasting into free spellcasting to solve the problems noted above. However, other features are required to make use of these rules, such as the _Cheapskate_ and _Trash Becomes Treasure_ [[Caster Training]] features.

**_Sunken Spells_**: Sunken Spells are spells that have a Spell Point cost below 0.

**_Depth_**: A Sunken Spell has a Depth equal to the number of [[Spell Points]] its cost is below 0 by.

**_Depthcasting_**: Depthcasting automatically happens when casting a Sunken Spell. When Depthcasting, the spell is upcast by a number of [[Spell Points]] equal to its Depth for free, bringing the casting cost back to 0.

**_Upcasting Sunken Spells_**: When you decide to upcast a Sunken Spell further, the casting cost is increased by the upcasting as usual, adding to the upcast facilitated through Depthcasting.
### Example
Through _Trash Becomes Treasure_, a level 1 Sorcerer has made their Evocation cantrips cost -2 Spell Points, leading to them being Sunken Spells with a Depth of 2. This means that whenever they cast an Evocation cantrip, it is automatically upcast by 2 Spell Points through Depthcasting. For their _Fire Bolt_, this leads to no additional effect by itself, however, they can now upcast their _Fire Bolt_ by 1 additional Spell Point to bring its casting cost to 1 Spell Point, being upcast by a total of 3 Spell Points, increasing its damage from 1d10 to 2d10. Starting with level 5, _Trash Becomes Treasure_ will reduce its cost by 3 instead, leading to this damage increase becoming automatic and free.
